Accessibility statement

Last updated 15 November 2026. This statement covers governorbrief.co.uk.

What we aim for

We want as many people as possible to be able to use this site. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA. At present the site is partially compliant: the known gaps are listed below.

What should work

  • Text and buttons have strong colour contrast, in both light and dark mode.
  • Body text is at least 16 pixels (a few short labels and tags are 15 pixels), and the pages reflow without sideways scrolling down to a width of 320 pixels.
  • Navigation, links, buttons and the enquiry and sign-up forms can be reached and used with a keyboard, with a visible focus outline.
  • Buttons and navigation links are at least 44 pixels high for touch.
  • Headings, lists, tables and form fields are marked up so that screen readers can announce them.
  • Form errors are listed at the top of the form and next to each field.
  • Briefings have a print layout, and the site respects the reduced-motion setting.

What we know does not work well

  • In the IDSR tool, the wider tables in a briefing scroll sideways inside their own box on a phone.
  • The one-page IDSR briefing is designed for A4 paper and uses small print. On screen you can zoom in, or use the full briefing or the Word copy, which use larger text.
  • The sample briefings are PDF files and have not been fully tagged for screen readers. The same content is available on screen in the IDSR tool by choosing Load example school.
  • The Word download has not been tested with every screen reader.

How this site was tested

The site is checked before each release with automated tests for text size, colour contrast of the palette, touch-target size, form errors and layout at phone, tablet and desktop widths, and with a manual keyboard check. It has not yet been tested with screen readers, and it has not had an independent accessibility audit or testing with disabled users.

Report a problem or ask for another format

Email enquiry@governorbrief.co.uk and say which page and what went wrong. We aim to reply within five working days.

If you are not happy with our response, the Equality Advisory and Support Service (EASS) gives advice on discrimination and accessibility: equalityadvisoryservice.com.
