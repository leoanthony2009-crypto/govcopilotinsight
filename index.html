The GovernorBrief Evidence-to-Impact Framework

The same four steps sit behind the tool, the briefings and the workshop.

  1. EvidenceUnderstand what the performance evidence shows, and what it does not.
  2. QuestionsTurn the evidence into a small number of purposeful questions.
  3. AssuranceAgree what would give the board confidence, and who will follow up.
  4. ImpactCome back to it: what has changed, and how do we know?

Who is behind GovernorBrief

GovernorBrief is run by Wendell Gopaul, a serving primary headteacher in Birmingham. Its purpose is to make effective governance more accessible, practical and evidence-informed.

  • Headteacher and designated safeguarding lead since 2016.
  • Ofsted inspector, 2014 to 2017.
  • Catholic school inspector since 2022.
  • Vice-chair of a multi-academy trust board, 2016 to 2018.

These are past and current roles. GovernorBrief is independent: it is not endorsed by, and does not speak for, Ofsted, the Catholic Schools Inspectorate, any diocese or trust, or his school or employer.

Independence and conflicts of interest. GovernorBrief is run in a personal capacity, separately from his school and employer. Paid work is not offered to his own school or to schools he inspects. Any possible conflict of interest is declared before a proposal is agreed, and paid work is confirmed only when any permissions that apply are in place.
