IDSR Governor Briefing

Turn your school's Ofsted IDSR into a one-page executive briefing, a full accessible briefing and an editable Word document, with headlines, an evidence trail and governor questions aligned to the 2026 inspection toolkit.

How to get the file
  1. Sign in to the IDSR site through DfE Sign-in and open your school's IDSR.
  2. Choose Download this page, or in your browser choose Save as… and pick Webpage, HTML only.
  3. Upload the saved .html file here. The PDF version will not work.

Data coverage
    Output

    Your data stays on this device. The IDSR is read in your browser only; nothing is sent to us or stored. You remain the data controller and Ofsted's IDSR conditions of use apply to anything you print or share.

    This briefing supports governor scrutiny. It does not replace the original IDSR or professional judgement, and it is not an Ofsted judgement, grade or prediction. Bandings and significance are reproduced from the IDSR; every headline carries a reference to its source row in the evidence trail. Small cohorts move percentages a lot: check the pupil numbers (n) before drawing conclusions.
