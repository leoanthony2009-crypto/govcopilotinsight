The GovernorBrief Evidence-to-Impact Framework

The same four steps sit behind the tool, the briefings and the workshop.

  1. EvidenceUnderstand what the performance evidence shows, and what it does not.
  2. QuestionsTurn the evidence into a small number of purposeful questions.
  3. AssuranceAgree what would give the board confidence, cross-check it against a second source, and decide who will follow up.
  4. ImpactCome back to it: what has changed, and how do we know?

Who is behind GovernorBrief

GovernorBrief is built on more than a decade of experience in school leadership and governance, including working directly with governing boards and serving at multi-academy trust board level.

Governors are given more information than any volunteer has time to read. GovernorBrief turns that information into the few questions that matter, so a board can tell the difference between being informed and being assured.

Every resource is built for the meeting you already have: a briefing you can read in three minutes, a tool that turns your school's IDSR into questions for governors, and a workshop where the whole board practises asking them.

GovernorBrief is currently in its pilot stage. The first schools and boards to use it benefit from pilot pricing and the opportunity to help shape how the service develops.
