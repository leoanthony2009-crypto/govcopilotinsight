Workshop for governing boards and trust boards

From Information to Assurance

A practical 90-minute face-to-face workshop to help governors move from receiving reports to asking better questions and securing meaningful follow-up.

  • 90 minutes
  • Face to face
  • At your school or venue
  • Pilot sessions in 2027
Request a workshop

Who it is for

  • Governors of maintained schools and academies, and trustees of multi-academy trusts.
  • Whole boards working together, including the chair, new governors and the governance professional or clerk.
  • Boards that receive plenty of information and want to be surer of what it tells them.

What your board leaves with

  • A shared way to read a data report in a few minutes and agree what matters.
  • A short set of purposeful questions to put to school leaders at the next meeting.
  • A simple way to record what was asked, what was answered and what follow-up was agreed.

The 90 minutes

Part 1 · about 25 minutes

Information

What a report such as the IDSR tells a board, what it does not, and how to find the few headlines that deserve the board's time.

Part 2 · about 35 minutes

Questions

Working in pairs on an invented example school, governors turn headlines into purposeful questions and test them on each other.

Part 3 · about 30 minutes

Assurance

How to listen to the answer, agree the follow-up and bring it back to the next meeting, so that scrutiny leads somewhere.

The session uses an invented example school. We do not need to see your school's IDSR, and we ask you never to email one to us.
