From Information to Assurance: Asking Better Questions

A practical 90-minute face-to-face workshop for governors and trustees.

Enquire about a workshop

Does your governing board receive reports but sometimes struggle to know what questions to ask, what evidence to expect or what should happen next?

The GovernorBrief Workshop helps governors develop confidence in understanding evidence, asking constructive questions and agreeing meaningful follow-up.

Learning outcomes

Participants will practise how to:

  • Distinguish information from evidence-based assurance.
  • Identify gaps or uncertainties in leadership reports.
  • Ask constructive questions that respect operational boundaries.
  • Connect meeting discussions to actions and follow-up.
  • Apply these skills to safeguarding, pupil outcomes and inclusion.

Workshop structure

  1. 0–10 minutesWhat does effective challenge sound like?
  2. 10–25 minutesReading reports through a governor's lens.
  3. 25–45 minutesSafeguarding and vulnerable pupil scenarios.
  4. 45–65 minutesThe three-question challenge.
  5. 65–80 minutesFrom discussion to board action.
  6. 80–90 minutesCommitments and next steps.

Practical details

Duration
90 minutes.
Format
Face-to-face twilight session at the school or an agreed venue.
When
After the school day, on a date agreed with you.
Participants
Ideally 6–20.

All scenarios used in the session are fictional or appropriately anonymised.

The session is adapted to your board. Responsibilities differ between maintained school governing bodies, academy trust boards and local governing bodies with delegated powers, so we ask which you are when you enquire.

What is included

  • Short pre-session discussion.
  • Facilitated workshop.
  • Better Questions Checklist.
  • Board Assurance Action Template.
  • Brief follow-up summary.
