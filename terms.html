What the tool is

GovernorBrief is an independent tool. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or produced by Ofsted or the Department for Education, and it does not replace the IDSR.

The tool restates the tables in a school's IDSR as headlines and questions, using fixed rules. It supports governor scrutiny. It is not an Ofsted judgement, grade or prediction, and it does not replace professional judgement. This is a pilot version.

Your responsibilities

  • Use the tool only with an IDSR you are authorised to handle.
  • Follow Ofsted's IDSR conditions of use for anything you print, save or share.
  • Check the briefing against the original IDSR before relying on it, and check small-number figures before sharing beyond the board.
  • Never email an IDSR to us.
