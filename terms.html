The IDSR tool

The tool restates the tables in a school's IDSR as headlines and questions, using fixed rules. It is a pilot version. It is not an Ofsted judgement, grade or prediction, it does not replace the IDSR or professional judgement, and it has not been validated against every IDSR format.

  • Use the tool only with an IDSR you are authorised to handle.
  • Follow Ofsted's IDSR conditions of use for anything you print, save or share.
  • Check the briefing against the original IDSR before relying on it, and check small-number figures before sharing beyond the board.
  • Never email an IDSR to us.
