4. The IDSR briefing tool

Where the IDSR tool is available, it is intended to help authorised users interpret selected information from an Ofsted Inspection Data Summary Report (IDSR).

The tool uses defined processing rules to produce draft briefings and suggested questions.

It does not independently verify the accuracy of the original IDSR or make an Ofsted judgement.

The tool may not support every IDSR format, school type, reporting year or data presentation. Any supported formats and known limitations will be identified on the relevant tool page.

Outputs should always be checked against the original IDSR before use.

4.1 Authorised use

You must only use an IDSR where you have the necessary authority from the school, trust or relevant data owner.

You must comply with Ofsted's IDSR conditions of use and storage, including requirements concerning educational purposes, authorised access, secure storage and restricted sharing.

You must not use the tool to access, process or distribute an IDSR without appropriate permission.

4.2 Confidentiality and safeguarding

IDSRs and generated briefings may contain confidential or sensitive school information.

You are responsible for:

  • Ensuring that only authorised people access the original IDSR and its outputs.
  • Keeping downloaded and printed materials secure.
  • Reviewing small-group figures and other potentially identifying information before sharing.
  • Following your school's or trust's data protection, information security and retention policies.
  • Obtaining any necessary permission before using the information in meetings or external activities.

Do not email real IDSR files, identifiable pupil information, safeguarding records or confidential personnel information to GovernorBrief.

4.3 Local processing

The current IDSR tool is designed to process selected files within the user's browser rather than uploading their contents to GovernorBrief's servers.

This description applies to the current implementation and should be read alongside the Privacy Notice.

Local processing does not remove the user's responsibility to protect files, downloads, browser sessions, printed copies or shared devices.

If the service changes to include server-side processing, accounts or cloud storage, the relevant documentation will be updated before those features are introduced.

4.4 Accuracy and limitations

The IDSR tool is provided as a developing educational aid.

Automated interpretation may be incomplete, inaccurate or unsuitable for a particular report.

Users must verify:

  • The reporting period and school context.
  • The source figures and referenced evidence.
  • The treatment of small cohorts and suppressed information.
  • Whether any data is missing or has been misinterpreted.
  • Whether the generated questions are relevant and proportionate.

GovernorBrief does not guarantee that the tool will process every valid IDSR or that generated outputs will be free from error.
