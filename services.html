WORKSHOP

From Information to Assurance

A 90-minute whole-board workshop, delivered face to face as a twilight session.

Who it is for

Governing boards of maintained schools and academies, trust boards and local governing bodies, working together as a whole board.

Common problems it addresses

  • The board receives reports but is unsure what questions to ask.
  • It is not clear what evidence the board should expect to see.
  • Discussion does not lead to agreed follow-up.

Learning outcomes

  • Distinguish information from evidence-based assurance.
  • Identify gaps or uncertainties in leadership reports.
  • Ask constructive questions that respect operational boundaries.
  • Connect meeting discussions to actions and follow-up.
  • Apply these skills to safeguarding, pupil outcomes and inclusion.

What the board receives

A short pre-session discussion, the facilitated workshop, a Better Questions Checklist, a Board Assurance Action Template and a brief follow-up summary.

Pilot price: £295 per board, subject to agreed scope, delivery arrangements and travel.

REVIEW

School Performance & Assurance Review

A tightly scoped review of school performance evidence, improvement priorities and governance assurance.

What the review includes

  • An initial scoping conversation.
  • Review of a limited, agreed evidence set.
  • Identification of key questions and priorities.
  • One structured discussion with school leadership and governance representatives.
  • A concise findings and assurance action note.

What the review is not

  • A full school evaluation.
  • An Ofsted inspection or a prediction of an inspection outcome.
  • A safeguarding audit.
  • Unlimited consultancy support.
  • A guarantee of improved school performance.

School leaders remain responsible for running the school, and the governing board for strategic oversight. The review supports both; it does not replace either.

Every review is subject to an agreed written scope and secure information-handling arrangements, settled before any school information is shared. Please do not send school data, or any pupil or staff information, through this website.

Proposed pilot price: £595 per school, subject to an agreed written scope.
