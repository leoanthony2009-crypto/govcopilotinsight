GovernorBrief logo

How to use it, on one page

Use the files as supplied. Do not retype the name or redraw the symbol. In the symbol the G and the B share one upright; the lettering is saved as shapes, so it looks the same on every computer.

1. The three versions

Primary logo. The default: letterheads, slides, documents, email signatures.

With strapline. Where it can be large: the website header and footer, covers, title slides, banners.

Symbol. Social profile pictures, app and browser icons.

2. Clear space

½ S½ S½ S½ S

Keep a margin of at least half the symbol's height (½ S) on every side. No text, pictures or page edges inside it.

If the strapline would be smaller than this, use the primary logo and set the strapline as ordinary text elsewhere on the page. The browser icon has heavier lines drawn for very small sizes; do not enlarge it.

On white or soft ivory (#F4F5F2) the logo is midnight teal with a digital teal B. On midnight teal it is white with a brass B. Brass is too pale to read on white, so never use the brass B, or brass text, on a light background. For one-colour printing use the files in brand/mono.

5. Please don't

  • stretch, squash, rotate or tilt the logo
  • change its colours, or add shadows, outlines or glows
  • place it on a photograph or a patterned background
  • move the symbol, the name or the strapline around
  • retype the name in another typeface
  • use the strapline version below its minimum size
