3 Minutes Before the Meeting · Briefing 01 · Under three minutes to read · Published November 2026

Are we getting assurance – or just information?

Governing boards receive reports on safeguarding, standards, attendance, finance and school improvement. Reports are useful, but receiving information is not the same as obtaining assurance.

Assurance comes from understanding the evidence, testing whether it is reliable and knowing how leaders respond when something is not working.

Look for

  • A clear explanation of what is happening, including concerns.
  • Evidence supporting the report, including appropriate external validation.
  • Actions, responsibilities and timescales where improvement is needed.

Three questions

  1. What evidence gives leaders confidence that this is working?
  2. What is the most significant concern or uncertainty in the evidence?
  3. What will be different by the next meeting, and how will we know?

Send to your board

One assurance check

Review the previous meeting's actions. Can the board identify what has changed, rather than simply whether an action was marked complete?

Governors should seek strategic assurance without taking over operational decisions.
