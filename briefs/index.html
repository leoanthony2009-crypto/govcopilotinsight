3 Minutes Before the Meeting

One issue. Three questions. One assurance check.

Short, practical briefings that help governors prepare for meetings and focus on what matters. Each takes about three minutes to read. No account or subscription is needed.

What every briefing contains

  • A plain-English explanation of the issue.
  • Two or three evidence signals to look for.
  • Three constructive questions for the meeting.
  • One assurance check to follow up.
  • A short reminder about the line between governance and operational management.
