Privacy notice

Last updated 15 November 2026.

This notice explains what personal information GovernorBrief handles, why, and what your rights are. It follows the transparency requirements of the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

Who we are

GovernorBrief is operated by Wendell Gopaul, who is the data controller for the personal information described here. To ask anything about this notice or your information, email enquiry@governorbrief.co.uk.

The short version

  • The IDSR tool never sends your school's IDSR, or anything in it, to us or to anyone else.
  • The site uses no cookies, no analytics and no advertising.
  • We hold personal information only if you send an enquiry or sign up for briefing emails, and we use it only for that purpose.

The IDSR tool: we receive nothing

When you choose an IDSR file, it is opened by your own browser on your own device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, and nothing from it is stored after you close the page. The site's security policy stops the tool page from sending data to any server in the background.

Your school or trust remains responsible for its IDSR and for anything you print or share from it. Ofsted's IDSR conditions of use apply.

Visiting the website

The site is hosted by Netlify. Like any web host, Netlify keeps ordinary server logs of visits, such as the IP address, the page asked for, the time and the type of browser. We use these only to keep the site running and secure, which is our legitimate interest. We do not use them to identify or profile visitors.

Enquiries

What we collectYour name, professional email address, school or organisation, the type of enquiry and anything you write in the message. The same applies if you email us instead of using the form.
WhyTo reply to you and, if you wish, to discuss and arrange a workshop.
Lawful basisLegitimate interests: answering an enquiry you have chosen to send. If a workshop is arranged, steps taken at your request before entering into a contract, and then the contract itself.
Do you have to provide it?No. But without contact details we cannot reply.
Who receives itNetlify, which receives the form, stores the submission and checks it for spam using the Akismet service. The submission is then emailed to a Microsoft 365 mailbox supplied through GoDaddy. Netlify and Microsoft (supplied through GoDaddy) act on our instructions as processors; Akismet is Netlify's sub-processor. We do not sell or share your details, and sending an enquiry does not add you to a mailing list.
Transfers outside the UKNetlify, Akismet, Microsoft and GoDaddy are based in the United States or operate there, so your details may be processed outside the UK. Where they are sent to the United States, the transfer relies on the UK–US data bridge (the UK Extension to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework) where the provider is certified, or otherwise on standard contractual clauses with the UK Addendum. Email us if you would like to know which applies or to see a copy.
How long we keep itEnquiries that do not lead to a workshop are deleted from the form store and the mailbox within 12 months of the last message. Records of work that goes ahead are kept for six years.
Automated decisionsNone, apart from automatic spam filtering.

Please never send an IDSR, or any information about individual pupils or staff, by form or by email. If one arrives, we will delete it and tell you.

Briefing emails

What we collectYour email address, if you sign up on the briefing emails page.
WhyTo email you when a new 3 Minutes Before the Meeting briefing is published. After you sign up we send one email asking you to confirm, and add you to the list only when you reply. Every email says how to stop. We do not use the address for anything else.
Lawful basisConsent, which you give by signing up and then confirming by reply. You can withdraw it at any time by replying to any of our emails or writing to enquiry@governorbrief.co.uk.
Do you have to provide it?No. The briefings are free to read on this site without signing up.
Who receives itThe same services as for enquiries: Netlify, which receives the form, stores the submission and checks it for spam using Akismet, and the Microsoft 365 mailbox supplied through GoDaddy, from which the emails are sent. We do not sell or share your address.
Transfers outside the UKAs for enquiries, above.
How long we keep itUntil you ask us to stop. We stop emailing you straight away and delete your address from the form store, the mailbox and the mailing list within one month. If you sign up but do not confirm, your address is deleted within one month. If the briefings come to an end, the list is deleted.
Automated decisionsNone, apart from automatic spam filtering.

School information

We do not collect school information through this website, and the workshop does not need it.

Your rights

Under data protection law you can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of the information we hold about you;
  • correct it if it is wrong;
  • delete it;
  • restrict how we use it, or object to our using it;
  • give it to you in a form you can pass to someone else, where that right applies.

Email enquiry@governorbrief.co.uk. We will reply within one month and there is no charge.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, please tell us first. We will acknowledge a complaint within 30 days. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk, telephone 0303 123 1113).

Children

This site is for adults involved in school governance. We do not knowingly collect information from or about children.
