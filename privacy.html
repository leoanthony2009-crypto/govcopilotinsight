Privacy notice

Last updated 14 November 2026.

Who we are

GovernorBrief is operated by Wendell Gopaul, who is the data controller for the personal information described here. Contact: enquiry@governorbrief.co.uk.

The briefing tool: we receive nothing

When you choose an IDSR file, it is opened by your own browser on your own device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, and nothing from it is stored after you close the page. The site's security policy stops the page from sending data to any server in the background. There are no accounts, cookies, analytics scripts or adverts.

Your school or trust remains responsible for its IDSR and for anything you print or share from it. Ofsted's IDSR conditions of use apply.

Visiting the website

The site is hosted by Netlify. Like any web host, Netlify keeps ordinary server logs of visits, such as the IP address, the page asked for, the time and the type of browser. We use these only to keep the site running and secure. Netlify is based in the United States and may process these logs there.

When you email us

What we holdYour name, role, organisation, email address, phone number if you give one, and what you tell us about your board.
WhyTo reply to you and, if you ask, to arrange and deliver a workshop.
Lawful basisOur legitimate interest in answering enquiries, and steps taken at your request before entering into a contract.
Where it is keptIn a Microsoft 365 mailbox supplied through GoDaddy. Both act as our processors.
How longEnquiries that do not lead to a booking are deleted within 12 months of the last message. Booking and payment records are kept for six years, as tax law requires.
Who sees itOnly us and the processors above. We do not sell or share your details, and we do not add you to a mailing list unless you ask.

Please never email an IDSR, or any information about individual pupils or staff, to us. If one arrives, we will delete it and tell you.

Your rights

You can ask to see the information we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, or to object to how we use it. Email enquiry@governorbrief.co.uk and we will reply within one month.

If you are unhappy with our answer, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes

If this notice changes, the new version will be published here with a new date.

GovernorBrief is an independent tool. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or produced by Ofsted or the Department for Education, and it does not replace the IDSR.
