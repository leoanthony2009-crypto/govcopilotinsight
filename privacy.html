Privacy notice

Last updated 19 November 2026.

This notice explains what personal information GovernorBrief handles, why, and what your rights are. It follows the transparency requirements of the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

Who we are

GovernorBrief is operated by Wendell Gopaul, who is the data controller for the personal information described here. To ask anything about this notice or your information, email enquiry@governorbrief.co.uk.

The short version

  • The IDSR tool never sends your school's IDSR, or anything in it, to us or to anyone else.
  • The site uses no cookies, no analytics and no advertising. If you sign up to use the IDSR tool, one small marker is saved in your own browser so that you are not asked again.
  • We hold personal information only if you send an enquiry, sign up to use the IDSR tool or sign up for briefing emails, and we use it only for that purpose.

Your school's IDSR: we receive nothing

When you choose an IDSR file, it is opened by your own browser on your own device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, and nothing from it is stored after you close the page. The site's security policy stops the tool page from sending data to any other site in the background, and the code contains nothing that sends it to ours.

Your school or trust remains responsible for its IDSR and for anything you print or share from it. Ofsted's IDSR conditions of use apply.

We do ask you to sign up before you first use the tool with your own file. That is a separate form on a separate page, described under Signing up to use the IDSR tool below, and it collects nothing from the IDSR.

Visiting the website

The site is hosted by Netlify. Like any web host, Netlify keeps ordinary server logs of visits, such as the IP address, the page asked for, the time and the type of browser. We use these only to keep the site running and secure, which is our legitimate interest. We do not use them to identify or profile visitors.

Enquiries

What we collectYour name, professional email address, school or organisation, the type of enquiry and anything you write in the message. If the link you followed to the form carried a short label, such as "offer-pdf", that label is sent with the form, so we can see which pages lead to enquiries and sign-ups; it says nothing about you. The same applies if you email us instead of using the form.
WhyTo reply to you and, if you wish, to discuss and arrange a workshop.
Lawful basisLegitimate interests: answering an enquiry you have chosen to send. If a workshop is arranged, steps taken at your request before entering into a contract, and then the contract itself.
Do you have to provide it?No. But without contact details we cannot reply.
Who receives itNetlify, which receives the form, stores the submission and checks it for spam using the Akismet service. The submission is then emailed to a Microsoft 365 mailbox supplied through GoDaddy. Netlify and Microsoft (supplied through GoDaddy) act on our instructions as processors; Akismet is Netlify's sub-processor. We do not sell or share your details, and sending an enquiry does not add you to a mailing list.
Transfers outside the UKNetlify, Akismet, Microsoft and GoDaddy are based in the United States or operate there, so your details may be processed outside the UK. Where they are sent to the United States, the transfer relies on the UK–US data bridge (the UK Extension to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework) where the provider is certified, or otherwise on standard contractual clauses with the UK Addendum. Email us if you would like to know which applies or to see a copy.
How long we keep itEnquiries that do not lead to a workshop are deleted from the form store and the mailbox at the first quarterly review after 12 months from the last message, so within 15 months at most. Records of work that goes ahead are kept for six years.
Automated decisionsNone, apart from automatic spam filtering.

Please never send an IDSR, or any information about individual pupils or staff, by form or by email. If one arrives, we will delete it and tell you.

Signing up to use the IDSR tool

Before you use the IDSR tool with your own school's file, we ask you to sign up once on the sign-up page. The example school and the sample briefings do not need a sign-up.

What we collectYour name, professional email address, school or trust, your role, your confirmation that you are authorised to handle your school's IDSR, whether you agreed to be emailed about the pilot, whether you agreed to be emailed about workshops, and the date. Netlify also records the time, IP address and browser type with the submission. We do not collect anything from the IDSR itself.
WhyTo know which schools and roles are using the pilot tool, so that we can improve it, and to keep a record that each user confirmed they are authorised. If you tick the box, we may also email you about the pilot, for example to ask for feedback. If you tick the separate box about workshops, we may email you about GovernorBrief workshops for governing boards. We do not use your sign-up details for anything else, and they are not used in any other role held by the person who runs GovernorBrief.
Lawful basisLegitimate interests: understanding who uses a pilot tool that handles confidential school data, and keeping a record of each user's confirmation. For emails about the pilot or about workshops: consent, which you give by ticking the box for each and can withdraw at any time by writing to enquiry@governorbrief.co.uk. You can object to our keeping your sign-up record at any time by emailing the same address; we will then delete it unless we have a compelling reason to keep it.
Do you have to provide it?No. But without it you cannot use the tool with your own file. Ticking either box about emails is optional.
Who receives itThe same services as for enquiries: Netlify, which receives the form, stores the submission and checks it for spam using Akismet, and the Microsoft 365 mailbox supplied through GoDaddy. We do not sell or share your details, and unless you tick a box, signing up does not add you to the briefing email list or any other mailing list.
Transfers outside the UKAs for enquiries, above.
How long we keep itFor 12 months from the date you sign up. It is then deleted from the form store and the mailbox at the next quarterly review, so within 15 months at most. If you ask us to remove your details, we do so within one month.
Automated decisionsNone, apart from automatic spam filtering.

A marker in your browser. When you sign up, the site saves one small marker in your browser's local storage, holding only the date you signed up. (While the sign-up form is being sent, a second, temporary marker is held for a moment and then removed.) The tool reads it so that it does not ask you to sign up again on the same device. It works like a cookie that is strictly necessary for a service you have asked for: it only remembers that you signed up. It is never sent to us or to anyone else, and it is not used to track you. Clearing your browsing data removes it; you would then be asked to sign up again.

Briefing emails

What we collectYour email address, if you sign up on the briefing emails page.
WhyTo email you when a new 3 Minutes Before the Meeting briefing is published. After you sign up we send one email asking you to confirm, and add you to the list only when you reply. Every email says how to stop. If you tick the separate box, we may also email you about GovernorBrief workshops for governing boards. We do not use the address for anything else.
Lawful basisConsent, which you give by signing up and then confirming by reply. You can withdraw it at any time by replying to any of our emails or writing to enquiry@governorbrief.co.uk.
Do you have to provide it?No. The briefings are free to read on this site without signing up.
Who receives itThe same services as for enquiries: Netlify, which receives the form, stores the submission and checks it for spam using Akismet, and the Microsoft 365 mailbox supplied through GoDaddy, from which the emails are sent. We do not sell or share your address.
Transfers outside the UKAs for enquiries, above.
How long we keep itUntil you ask us to stop. We stop emailing you straight away and delete your address from the form store, the mailbox and the mailing list within one month. If you sign up but do not confirm, your address is deleted at the next monthly check, so within two months at most. If the briefings come to an end, the list is deleted.
Automated decisionsNone, apart from automatic spam filtering.

School information

Apart from the name of your school or organisation on the forms described above, we do not collect school information through this website, and the workshop does not need it.

Your rights

Under data protection law you can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of the information we hold about you;
  • correct it if it is wrong;
  • delete it;
  • restrict how we use it, or object to our using it;
  • give it to you in a form you can pass to someone else, where that right applies.

Email enquiry@governorbrief.co.uk. We will reply within one month and there is no charge.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, please tell us first. We will acknowledge a complaint within 30 days. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk, telephone 0303 123 1113).

Children

This site is for adults involved in school governance. We do not knowingly collect information from or about children.
