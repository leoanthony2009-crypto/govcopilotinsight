THE WORKSHOP

Asking Better Questions

Does your board receive reports but sometimes struggle to know what to ask, what evidence to expect or what should happen next? This 90-minute session gives the whole board practice.

Your board will practise how to

  • Distinguish information from evidence-based assurance.
  • Identify gaps or uncertainties in leadership reports.
  • Ask constructive questions that respect operational boundaries.
  • Connect meeting discussions to actions and follow-up.
  • Apply these skills to safeguarding, pupil outcomes and inclusion.

What is included

  • Short pre-session discussion.
  • Facilitated 90-minute workshop.
  • Better Questions Checklist.
  • Board Assurance Action Template.
  • Brief follow-up summary.

Practical details

Format
Face-to-face twilight session at your school or an agreed venue.
Where
Birmingham and the West Midlands; elsewhere by arrangement.
Who
Governors and trustees, ideally 6–20, with the chair and the clerk where possible.
Data
None needed. Scenarios are fictional or anonymised.

Who is behind GovernorBrief

GovernorBrief is led by Wendell Gopaul, a serving primary headteacher in Birmingham who has worked with governing boards for more than ten years, as a headteacher reporting to one and as vice-chair of a multi-academy trust board. It turns the information governors are given into the few questions that matter, so a board can tell the difference between being informed and being assured. GovernorBrief is in its pilot stage.
