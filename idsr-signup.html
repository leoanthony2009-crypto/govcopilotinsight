Sign up to use the IDSR tool

The tool is free during the pilot. Tell us who you are once, then use it with your own school's file.

Your details

This takes about a minute. There is no password and no account to manage.

There is a problem

    Your answers are sent to GovernorBrief through our website host, Netlify. We use them to know which schools and roles are using the pilot tool and to keep a record of your confirmation. We email you about the pilot only if you tick the box above, and signing up does not add you to any mailing list. Your school's IDSR is never sent to us. See the privacy notice and the terms of use.

    Why we ask

    • The tool is a pilot. Knowing which schools and roles use it helps us improve it.
    • An IDSR is confidential. We ask each user to confirm they are entitled to handle their school's.
    • Your IDSR is still read only in your own browser. Signing up does not change that.

    This browser remembers that you have signed up, so you are usually asked only once on this device. A different device or browser will ask again, and so will this one if its browsing data is cleared.

    Want to look first? Load the example school in the tool, or see the sample briefing (PDF). Neither needs a sign-up.
