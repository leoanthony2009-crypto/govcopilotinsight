There is a problem

    By signing up you agree that GovernorBrief may email you when a new briefing is published. We will first send one email asking you to confirm, and add you to the list only when you reply. Unless you tick the box about workshops, we use your address for nothing else. We do not share it. You can stop at any time by replying to any of our emails or writing to enquiry@governorbrief.co.uk. Your address is sent to us through our website host, Netlify. See the privacy notice.

    What you will receive

    • An email with a link to each new briefing when it is published.
    • One issue, three questions and one assurance check, ready for your next meeting.
    • No advertising, and no emails about anything else.
